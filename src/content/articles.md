# Scholarship in Public
Faculty are asked to teach, publish, advise, serve on committees, and carry their institutions through every crisis, and much of that work goes unrecorded. Addrain Conyers (C3), who became Interim Provost and Dean of the Faculty at Marist University on July 1, 2026, has spent his doctoral research on what the gap does to people. This summer, _Innovative Higher Education_ accepted [“The Contributions–Impact Model of Faculty Burnout,”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10755-026-09952-y) drawn from his dissertation; it appeared open access on September 4. The model separates what faculty contribute from the impact of that work and from the recognition it earns, and it gives a provost, as much as a researcher, a way to see where the three come apart.

Others in the program put questions about equity, admissions, artificial intelligence, labor-market data, and the flow of students from China into print, onto panels, and in front of policymakers.

# Leading Change
Massachusetts now lets a college try a three-year bachelor’s degree, on conditions. Kristen Stone (C1), working with the Department of Higher Education’s legal and academic affairs teams, co-drafted [610 CMR 16.00](https://www.mass.edu/bhe/lib/documents/BHE/BHE-26-42-BHE-Motion-Approving-610-CMR-16.00-for-Promulgation.pdf), the regulation the Board of Higher Education [adopted on February 10, 2026](https://www.mass.gov/news/massachusetts-opens-the-door-to-three-year-degrees-and-other-innovative-approaches-to-higher-education) to govern pilot proposals on innovation. A college may test a new model; in return the program must be called an applied bachelor’s degree, students must sign a disclosure acknowledging its pilot status, and the institution must report to the Board every year. On June 26 the Board [approved the first two pilots](https://www.mass.gov/news/massachusetts-approves-first-three-year-bachelors-degree-pilot-programs-adding-to-affordable-college-options): 96-credit applied bachelor’s degrees in business administration, communications, criminal justice, and psychology at Merrimack College, and a 94-credit applied bachelor’s degree in healthcare administration and innovation at Suffolk University, both starting in fall 2027.

A rule is one way a value becomes something other people can use and be held to. Partnerships, archives, and strategy are others.

A regulation can become compliance without learning; a partnership, branding without reciprocity; an archive, a collection nobody can reach. Each structure here was built with co-drafters, boards, partner schools, archivists, and communities, and each will hold only as long as someone stays answerable for it. Once a commitment is durable enough that others can act through it, the question changes: who will be responsible for what the institution has built?
# Towards the Greater
On July 1, 2026, [Addrain Conyers (C3) became Interim Provost and Dean of the Faculty at Marist University](https://www.marist.edu/social-behavioral-science/faculty/addrain-conyers) and [Keith Maczkiewicz, SJ (C1) became Vice President for Mission and Chief Mission Officer at the College of the Holy Cross](https://magazine.holycross.edu/stories/holy-cross-names-rev-keith-maczkiewicz-sj-edd-vice-president-mission-and-chief-mission). One now sets the conditions of academic life for a faculty; the other has to make a Jesuit mission show up in hiring and budget decisions and not only in the mission statement. A larger title enlarges the reach of one person’s judgment across faculty lives, student opportunities, budgets, and purpose.
## A provost, a mission officer, an interim dean
Conyers arrives with his [burnout model](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10755-026-09952-y) in print. A provost sets workloads, service expectations, and what counts at promotion, which is where the gap his research measures is made or closed. Maczkiewicz’s office exists for the moments when priorities compete, so that a community can defend its choices in its own language. Budgets decide what survives. International strategy decides which relationships cross borders. Academic administration decides what faculty and students can do on a given Tuesday. None of these offices guarantees good judgment, and each can pull a leader away from the people its decisions touch. What every new seat does offer is a new view of whose flourishing is at stake, and the obligation to keep those people in sight.
